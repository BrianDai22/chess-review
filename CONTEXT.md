# Chess Review

A personal chess learning tool for reviewing completed games and discussing their positions with a coach.

## Language

**Game**:
A completed chess game, including its played moves, players, result, and time control.
_Avoid_: Match

**Position**:
A particular state of a game or variation, including the piece placement, side to move, and rights that affect legal moves.
_Avoid_: Board, when referring to the full state rather than its visual display

**Played line**:
The sequence of moves actually played in a game.
_Avoid_: Main line, which can also mean a recommended continuation

**Variation**:
A sequence of alternative legal moves explored from a position. A variation is distinct from the played line.
_Avoid_: Edited game

**Analysis**:
An assessment of a position and its possible continuations, including evaluations and supporting moves.
_Avoid_: Review, when referring only to the assessment

**Review**:
A guided examination of a completed game, with move assessments, important moments, explanations, and opportunities to retry decisions.
_Avoid_: Analysis, when referring to the guided learning experience

**Move classification**:
A named assessment of a played move, such as Mistake, Blunder, or Brilliant, under a specified set of classification rules.
_Avoid_: Rating, which describes a player's playing strength

**Accuracy score**:
A summary of move quality across a game under a specified scoring method. It is distinct from a player's rating and from an estimate of winning chances.
_Avoid_: Rating, win percentage

**Coach chat**:
A conversation connected to a review session, supporting follow-up questions and exploration of alternative moves and plans.
_Avoid_: Review, when referring to the conversation rather than the whole learning experience

**Review session**:
The particular open review that a coaching conversation is connected to, including its selected position and explored variation.
_Avoid_: Game, when referring to the live review rather than the completed game

**Current review context**:
The current game, selected position, variation, and analysis evidence belonging to a connected review session.
_Avoid_: Snapshot handoff, when referring to a live connection

**Canonical review score**:
The authoritative score computed by the tool's single scoring method and used throughout the review and coaching discussion.
_Avoid_: Alternate score, imported accuracy, when describing the score shown by this tool

**Plan**:
A position-specific goal and the preparation needed to pursue it, including the opponent's counterplay and conditions for reconsidering the goal.
_Avoid_: Forced line, unless the continuation is actually forced

**Key moment**:
A position selected for focused learning because it contains a consequential mistake, missed opportunity, or useful planning decision.
_Avoid_: Blunder, when the position is valuable for reasons other than a blunder

**Review evidence**:
The position facts, checked continuations, and move assessments that support an explanation about a game.
_Avoid_: Explanation, when referring to its supporting facts rather than the coach's interpretation

**Retry outcome**:
The result of attempting a decision again, including the chosen move, its assessed quality, and whether help was shown before the attempt.
_Avoid_: Improvement, when referring to one attempt rather than a supported trend
